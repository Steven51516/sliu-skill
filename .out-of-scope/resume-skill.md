# A `resume` skill

There is no skill for resuming a project or a session.

## Why this is out of scope

State lives in files: the map, its tickets (each with a dated "Where I stopped" when left unfinished), `STATUS.md` and
`research-status --now`. Any fresh session picks up from them, which is the point of keeping the driver's context small.
A resume skill would duplicate what the files and the tracker already say, and Matt Pocock's skills have none.
